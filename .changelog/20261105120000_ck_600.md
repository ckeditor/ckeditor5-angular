Added support for the multi-root editor with the `<ckeditor-multi-root>` component (`CKEditorMultiRootComponent`). It binds the data and the attributes of all roots with `[(data)]` and `[(rootsAttributes)]`, and adds or removes roots with `addRoot()` and `removeRoot()`. Without content, it renders the toolbar and the editables of all roots. For a custom layout, place `<ckeditor-multi-root-toolbar>`, `<ckeditor-multi-root-menu-bar>`, and `<ckeditor-multi-root-editable>` inside it yourself.

**This feature is in early access.** Its API may change without a major release.
